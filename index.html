Sign out

These tools are writing and recording aids, not clinical decision tools. Check every note, dictation, chart and design before it goes into the record or to the lab, treat smile designs as a 2D simulation, not a promised outcome, treat digital wax-ups as a guide for the lab (the technician designs the final restoration in their own software), check every special tray and record base on the cast or in the mouth before you rely on it, and confirm implant torque and compatibility against the current manufacturer IFU, and open the source document before relying on any guideline or classification summary; the clinician remains responsible for its accuracy. Do not enter patient names, CHI or hospital numbers.
