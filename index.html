Sign out

These tools are writing and recording aids, not clinical decision tools. Check every note, dictation, chart and design before it goes into the record or to the lab, treat smile designs as a 2D simulation, not a promised outcome, and confirm implant torque and compatibility against the current manufacturer IFU, and open the source document before relying on any guideline or classification summary; the clinician remains responsible for its accuracy. Do not enter patient names, CHI or hospital numbers.
