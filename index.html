Sign out

These tools are writing and recording aids, not clinical decision tools. Check every note, chart and design before it goes into the record or to the lab, and treat smile designs as a 2D simulation, not a promised outcome; the clinician remains responsible for its accuracy. Do not enter patient names, CHI or hospital numbers.
