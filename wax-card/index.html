Using the brushes

Pencil or mouse sculpts; fingers orbit, pinch and pan. With a mouse, drag on empty space to orbit and right-drag to pan. Adapt pulls the cervical edge of a tooth onto the upper scan so the wax-up and model merge into one piece.
