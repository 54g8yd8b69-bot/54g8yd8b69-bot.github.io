Press Ctrl + Space or the mic to start and stop (Esc also stops). Text goes in at the cursor and is copied, ready to paste into your notes.
Spoken commands and conversions
  • full stop
  • comma
  • question mark
  • colon / semicolon
  • new line
  • new paragraph
  • open / close bracket
  • “upper left six” → UL6
  • “G.I.C.” → GIC

Whisper adds most punctuation by itself, so the commands are mainly for forcing layout.

Custom vocabulary

One rule per line: heard => replace with. Rules ignore capitals and match whole words. Use them for terms Dictate keeps getting wrong. Saved on this device only.

Accurate uses Whisper base.en (about 100 MB, downloaded once). Fast uses Whisper tiny.en (about 40 MB): roughly twice as fast, a little less accurate. The mode also applies to Notile's Dictate buttons. Both run in this browser from files on this site; after the first download they work offline. Audio is held in memory only while it's transcribed. It is never saved or sent anywhere.

Dictate is a writing aid, not a clinical record. Check every phrase before it goes into the notes; the clinician remains responsible for its accuracy. Do not dictate patient names, CHI or hospital numbers.
