Snip Card On-device · images never leave this computer
Paste a screenshot, or choose an image.

On this computer: press Win + Shift + S to snip part of the screen, then Ctrl + V here. The text is read and copied, ready to paste into your notes.

Drag over the text you want.
Ways to get an image in
  • Capture screen: choose a window or screen to take one still picture of. Sharing stops straight away.
  • Paste: snip with Win + Shift + S (Windows) or Ctrl + Cmd + Shift + 4 (Mac), then Ctrl / Cmd + V on this page.
  • Drop or choose an image file. On a phone, Choose image also offers the camera.
Getting a clean read
  • Drag a box that holds only the text you want, with a little space around it.
  • Small text reads better if you zoom in on it before taking the screenshot.
  • Printed and on-screen text reads well. Handwriting does not.
  • Turn off Keep line breaks to join wrapped lines into paragraphs.

The reading engine (about 7 MB) downloads the first time and is then kept by the browser. English only.

Snip Card is a copying aid. Check the text against the original before it goes into the notes: letters and numbers can be misread (0 and O, 1 and l, 5 and S). The image and the text are held only while this page is open, and nothing is sent anywhere. Follow your organisation’s policy on capturing patient information.
