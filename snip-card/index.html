Ways to get text in
  • Snip and come back: turn on auto-read, snip with , then switch back to this page. The text is read and copied.
  • Paste: snip with , then on this page. The whole snip is read straight away.
  • Mini window: Pop out keeps a small Snip Card window on top of your other apps. Snip, then click Read snip in it, and paste where you need the text.
  • Capture screen: choose a window or screen to take one still picture of, then drag over the text. Sharing stops straight away unless you tick Keep sharing: then press Alt + S on this page for a fresh picture, and press Stop sharing when you are done.
  • Drop or choose an image file, then drag over the text. On a phone, Choose image also offers the camera.
  • To read just part of a snip again, drag over that part of the picture.
A real keyboard shortcut, in any app

A web page cannot hear keys pressed in other apps, and cannot look at the screen without asking each time. For one key press that works anywhere, like TextSniper, use what your computer already has:

Windows

PowerToys Text Extractor is the closest match: press Win + Shift + T, drag over any text and it is on the clipboard. PowerToys is a free Microsoft download, so on a trust computer you may need IT to install it.

Snipping Tool: on recent Windows 11 updates, press Win + Shift + S and choose Text extractor on the snipping bar. On older versions, open the snip and use Text actions, then Copy all text.

Or Snip Card: press Win + Shift + S, then come back to this page with auto-read on.

Mac

Make your own in Shortcuts (free, built in): make a new shortcut with the actions Take Screenshot (set to Interactive, and to a custom selection under Show More), Extract Text from Image, Combine Text (with New Lines) and Copy to Clipboard. Then open the shortcut’s details and choose Add Keyboard Shortcut. macOS may ask for permission the first time it runs.

Live Text: open a screenshot or photo in Preview or Photos and select the text as if it were typed.

iPhone and iPad

Live Text: in Photos or the Camera, tap the Live Text button, then Copy. Turn it on in Settings, General, Language & Region.

Getting a clean read
  • Snip only the text you want, with a little space around it.
  • Small text reads better if you zoom in on it before you snip.
  • Printed and on-screen text reads well. Handwriting does not.
  • Turn off Keep line breaks to join wrapped lines into paragraphs.

The reading engine (about 7 MB) downloads the first time and is then kept by the browser. English only.

Snip Card is a copying aid. Check the text against the original before it goes into the notes: letters and numbers can be misread (0 and O, 1 and l, 5 and S). The image and the text are held only while this page is open, and nothing is sent anywhere. Auto-read looks at the clipboard only when you come back to this page, and only reads pictures.
